PATHOWATCH

"Web platform designed to analyze and predict the spread of infectious diseases and epidemics. PathoWatch uses epidemiological data and statistical models to visualize disease transmission and support outbreak prevention and preparedness."

PathoWatch was developed as our final course project, using Angular for the frontend, FastAPI for the backend, and MariaDB as the database. The platform collects information about new disease outbreaks through web scraping from different sources, including the United Nations, and stores the collected data in the database. It also allows users to simulate disease outbreaks using epidemiological models such as SIR, SEIR, and SIRS, generating interactive visualizations with D3. The goal of the platform is to monitor new outbreaks, analyze disease transmission patterns, and explore how epidemics can develop under different simulated conditions.
