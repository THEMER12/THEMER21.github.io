MUSIC PLATFORM

"Personal project created to combine my passion for music with web development. A fully frontend turntable experience built from scratch, focusing on interaction, visual design, and creating a realistic way to enjoy music through the browser."

The project works with FLAC music files and focuses on creating an interactive listening experience with synchronized lyrics and audio visualizations. To generate the lyrics, I use the Whisper model to transcribe each song and obtain the corresponding timestamps. The generated lyrics and timing information are then stored and processed with JavaScript, allowing the lyrics to be displayed and synchronized with the music in real time. I also process the audio to generate decibel levels throughout each song. These values are used to create a background visualizer that reacts to the music while it is playing, adding a more dynamic and immersive experience.
